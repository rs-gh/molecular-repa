Object identity      & tokens of the same molecule or protein & within- minus between-object cosine similarity \\

\addlinespace
\multicolumn{3}{@{}p{\linewidth}@{}}{\textbf{Relevance}\quad\textit{what the generator can match without improving its representation}} \\
Saturation & the embeddings barely vary, so a constant prediction already matches them & a high \emph{floor}: the cosine similarity of the constant (dataset-mean) prediction \\
Cheatability     & the variation that exists is content the generator is already fed & a high \emph{lift} from identity alone: the rise a REPA-form 3-layer MLP gains from a one-hot of the atom or residue type (cosine-similarity loss, 80/20 split) \\
Off-task risk    & the remaining variation is some factor the generator does not need, rather than the 3D geometry it does & a low floor and low lift, read against the 3D-awareness probe \\

Sparsity, dead dims  & degenerate dimensions                 & fraction of exact zeros; permanently inactive dimensions \\
Norm scale           & numerical conditioning                & mean embedding $\ell_2$ norm \\

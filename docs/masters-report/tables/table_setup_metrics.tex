\caption{\textbf{We measure three things: representation quality, representation alignment, and generation quality.} Representation quality is the linear-probe recoverability of structural
labels from the trunk --- a per-residue battery (inverse-folding sequence recovery and
backbone dihedral error) and a per-chain fold probe on CATH, for which we report
\emph{architecture} (CATH-A) as the headline level (Table~\ref{tab:setup-cath}).
Representation alignment is CKNNA~\cite{PlatonicRep}, the mutual-nearest-neighbour
kernel-alignment between a trunk layer and an encoder. Generation quality is the
Chapter~\ref{ch:evaluation} supercolumn suite, which decomposes into per-sample
\emph{quality}, whole-set vs.\ designable-subset \emph{tertiary} structure (T-W, T-D),
and whole-set vs.\ designable-subset \emph{secondary} structure (S-W, S-D); arrows give
the good direction for each member metric, and the full per-metric breakdown appears in
Tables~\ref{tab:proteina-700k}--\ref{tab:proteina-1m-reduced}.}
